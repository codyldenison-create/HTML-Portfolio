Do You Like Funky Beats, Dancy Vibes, and Fun-Filled Crowd Work?

Then you need to see Minka live!

If you weren't at Rubber Gloves Denton last night, November 17h you missed a must-see show! (But there is still time.)

Hailing from Philladelphia, Minka has been delivering the sweet sounds of 80's inspired synth-funk since their 2019 debut album Reincarnation. Self-described as a scientific experiment combination of Bowie, Prince, and a sprinkle of Talking Heads, Minka delivered an exceptional performance at Denton's Rubber Gloves Rehearsal Studio.

The show met and exceeded expectations. The atmosphere was electric with out of this world synths, an energetic stage presence only Minka's lead could deliver, and a tenacious, groovy accompaniment. Minka's lead singer captivated the crowd with his flamboyant dance and crowd work, and even took to the floor to dance with the audience mid-song. The lead guitarist, with his glorious rad hair shredded his guitar like nobody's business all without lossing his precariously perched glasses. The bassist with his controlled demeanor offered a cool levelheaded contrast to the energetic lead. The drummer was the embodiment of unwavering focus, continuing to lay down the beat even as an LED sign fell on him mid-song. After confirming the drummer was okay, the show went on keeping show-goers dancing their worries away for an unforgettable night.

If you missed the show, or just want to see more, Minka is on tour and coming to a city near you! Get tickets now.
